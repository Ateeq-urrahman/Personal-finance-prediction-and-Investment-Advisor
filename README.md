Financial literacy and effective money management remain significant challenges for 
individuals across all income levels. The Personal Finance Prediction & Investment Advisor 
leverages artificial intelligence, machine learning, and big data analytics to provide 
personalised financial guidance, predict future financial trends, and recommend optimal 
investment strategies. This intelligent system analyses spending patterns, income streams, 
market conditions, and individual goals to create customised financial plans that adapt to 
changing circumstances, helping users achieve financial stability and long-term wealth 
building. Many individuals struggle with financial planning due to complexity, lack of 
expertise, and emotional biases in decision-making. Traditional financial advisors are 
expensive and inaccessible to average consumers, while generic financial advice fails to 
account for individual circumstances, risk tolerance, and life goals. People often lack visibility 
into spending patterns, miss investment opportunities, and fail to prepare for future 
expenses or emergencies.
